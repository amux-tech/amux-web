About AMUX

Technology should make real things work better.

AMUX is a developing product-based technology company. We look for practical problems and build considered digital solutions that can be useful in everyday work and life.

Our direction

AMUX is the company behind products designed around genuine needs. We are not tied to one industry, customer segment, or technology. Each opportunity starts with understanding the people and context involved, then deciding whether a focused product can make a meaningful difference.

Software is our current area of work. As the company develops, we remain open to exploring other technologies—including hardware—when the problem calls for it.

Our goal is straightforward: build useful products, be clear about what they do, and keep improving them with care.

What guides our work

Principles that keep the work grounded.

01

Start with people

Understand who experiences the problem and what a better outcome would mean for them.

02

Make useful choices

Build what serves the need, with clear priorities and no unnecessary complexity.

03

Keep earning trust

Communicate honestly, respect users, and take reliability and accessibility seriously.
