Privacy

A clear note on information you share.

This notice applies to the AMUX website and its email-based inquiry forms.

Information you provide

Contact, problem, and career application forms are handled in your browser. When you submit a form, the website prepares a message for your email application. The website does not upload or store the information you enter.

Career application resume

A resume selected on the careers page is not transferred to AMUX by the website. Your email application opens with the resume filename in the message. You must attach the resume yourself and send the email to helloamux@gmail.com.

Email communication

If you choose to send a message, the information in it is delivered to AMUX through your email provider and may be retained as needed to respond to you. Do not include sensitive personal information that is not needed for your inquiry.

Third-party services

This site may load web fonts from Google Fonts. Those requests are subject to the provider's own terms and privacy practices. No analytics or form-submission backend is used by the inquiry flows on this website.

Questions

For questions about this notice, contact helloamux@gmail.com.
