Problems we solve

Look closely at the friction. Build for a better way.

AMUX is open to problems across industries and technologies. These themes describe the kinds of practical needs that can lead to thoughtful product work.

01

Repeated manual work

Tasks that consume time, create avoidable errors, or make routine work harder than it needs to be.

02

Technical skill evaluation

Challenges in assessing, practicing, or demonstrating real technical capability consistently.

03

Disconnected workflows

Processes and tools that do not work together clearly, leaving people to bridge the gaps themselves.

04

Hard-to-use systems

Digital experiences that add complexity instead of helping people complete the task in front of them.

05

Limited access or clarity

Situations where a more accessible, understandable solution could help people participate more fully.

06

Emerging needs

New challenges where careful investigation can reveal whether software or another technology is the right fit.

These are examples, not a fixed company boundary. If you see an important problem, tell us about it.
